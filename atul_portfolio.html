About me

I turn data into insights, and insights into better decisions.

I’m an M.Tech student in Industrial Engineering at MNIT Jaipur, interested in the intersection of data analytics, statistics, and industrial problem-solving.

My approach is simple: understand the problem first, explore what the data is telling us, and use evidence to make better decisions. From writing SQL queries and performing statistical analysis to building Python dashboards, I enjoy turning raw data into something meaningful and useful.

Currently, I’m exploring how analytics can solve practical business and manufacturing challenges, with a particular interest in quality, reliability, and process improvement. My work also includes a BIS standards gap-analysis research project, where I’m looking at how statistical methods can better align with modern industry practices.

Beyond the numbers, I’m curious about how things work, enjoy learning by building, and believe the best solutions come from asking better questions.
